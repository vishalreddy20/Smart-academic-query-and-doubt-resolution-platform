Editorial Tips

  • 1

    Context is Queen

    Don't just post the question. Explain the broader concept you're studying so we can find the right specialist.

  • 2

    Visual Evidence

    Upload handwritten notes or diagrams. Visual representations often clarify doubts faster than text alone.

  • 3

    Be Specific

    Avoid "I don't understand this." Try "I understand Step A, but I don't see the logic connecting to Step B."
